# Learning Process

## Feynman Technique:
The Feynman Technique is a learning method where we understand a concept deeply by explaining it in simple words as if teaching a kid.

---

## What was the most interesting story or idea in the TED Talk?
The most interesting idea was that taking breaks and allowing the brain to relax can help us understand difficult concepts. Learning is not only about continuous studying; taking breaks also helps improve understanding.


## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?


- Active thinking: Concentrate deeply on a specific problem or task.
- Diffused thinking: When we face some problem while learning, relax the mind and allow it to connect ideas and find solutions naturally.

## What are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?
1. Choose a topic you want to learn.
2. Focus on one skill at a time.
3. Define your target performance level.
4. Break the topic into smaller parts.
5. Gather the necessary resources.
6. Remove distractions.
7. Spend time practising.
8. Get feedback and improve.

## What actions can you take to improve your learning process?
1. Need 100% attention while learning and practising.
2. No phone and other distractions - during study time.
3. Understand concepts instead of memorising them.
4. Explain each and every concept in own words.
5. Practice coding regularly and complete assignments on time.
6. Apply what I learn through small projects.
7. Track my learning progress and manage my time.
8. Ask questions and communicate difficulties proactively.
9. Take short breaks to improve concentration.
10. Revise concepts regularly.
