# GRIT and GROWTH Mindset

## 1. GRIT
Success if not only about talent or intelligence, instead it is also about having passion and perseverance.

Keep working towards the goals, even when the process becomes difficult. Consistency and Determination on the work leads to success.



## 2. Growth Mindset

A Growth Mindset is the belief that we can improve our skills and intelligence through learning, practice, and hardwork. It encourages us to accept challenges, englearn from mistakes, and see failure as opportunities to grow rather than giving up.


## 3. Internal Locus of Control
We should focus on things within our control rather than blaming external circumstances. it encourages us to take responsibility for out actions, decisions and efforts because our motivation and success depend on what we choose to do.

## 4. How to build a Growth Mindset
Start believing in your ability to figure things out and continuously improve yourself.

-   Challenge ourselves
-   Accept Struggles
-   Accept criticism
-   Learn independently
-   Focus on becoming better everyday
-   Avoiding difficulties
