# Prevention of Sexual Harassment
**PoSH** means Prevention of Sexual Harassment at the workplace.
## 1. What behaviour causes sexual harassment?
Sexual harassment can include:
- Unwanted touching, hugging or physical contact.
- Making sexual comments, jokes or gestures.
- Asking for sexual favours.
- Sending unwanted sexual messages, photos or videos.
- Staring or making someone feel uncomfortable with sexual behaviour.
- Showing sexual images or content.
- Repeatedly asking someone for dates after they say no.
- Threatening someone or promising benefits in return for sexual favours.

Remember: Even a single incident can be sexual harassment. It does not have to happen repeatedly.

## 2. What should you do if you face or witness harassment?
- Speak up: Clearly say that the behaviour is unwanted, if it is safe to do so.
- Move away: Leave the situation if you feel unsafe.
- Record details: Note the date, time, place and what happened.
- Keep evidence: Save messages, emails, photos or other proof.
- Report it: Inform HR, your manager or the company's Internal Committee (IC).
- Support the victim: Listen, believe their concerns and help them report the incident.
- Maintain privacy: Do not spread the incident as gossip.
- Don't ignore repeated behaviour: Report it even if it happens again after being asked to stop.

Remember: Everyone deserves a safe and respectful workplace. Sexual harassment is never acceptable.
